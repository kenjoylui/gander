PDFs have a night mode, from the viewer's menu. Paper goes black and text goes white, colours keep their hue, and photographs are left exactly as they were printed. Zooming into a PDF redraws the page at the zoom you are at, so a map or a plan stays sharp however far in you go. A document short enough to fit on the screen sits in the middle of it rather than at the top, and a Word page fills the width of the screen the way a PDF page and a slide always have.
